# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We aim to make participation in Muslim Apps Assistant welcoming and respectful for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or point of view.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate, constructive, and respectful in discussions and reviews.
- Critique ideas and code without attacking people.
- Accept feedback gracefully and help keep conversations focused on the project.
- Respect privacy. Do not publish another person's personal information or credentials.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, insults, discrimination, threats, unwanted sexual attention, doxxing, and repeated disruptive behavior are not acceptable. Do not use project spaces to target or demean people or groups.

## Handling concerns

If someone behaves unacceptably, ask them to stop when it is safe and appropriate. Report serious or persistent concerns privately to the repository owner through [GitHub](https://github.com/cooolv). Reports will be reviewed and handled as fairly and confidentially as possible.

Project maintainers may remove content or restrict participation when necessary to protect contributors and keep project spaces constructive.
